Hexly Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Nox Studios, LLC handles information in Hexly on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and in Hexly Daily at hexlydaily.com. If you have questions, email support@noxstudios.net.

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Hexly mobile app

This section covers Hexly on iPhone and iPad (iOS) and on Android. Android is coming soon. The Google-connected features described below apply when available in the Android release.

Overview and accounts

Hexly does not create or require a separate Hexly user account. Core gameplay works without Game Center or Google Play Games sign-in. Progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Optional connected features and advertising may transmit information to the service providers described below; not all information remains on-device.

Apple or Google handles purchases and authentication for its connected features through your existing platform account. Signing in to those services does not create a Hexly account.

Information Hexly may store

  • Local gameplay data: puzzle progress, completion records, solve times, streaks, Precision Mode results, and preferences.
  • Premium and Pack ownership: product identifiers and purchase status used to unlock content and restore purchases. The relevant store processes transaction information.
  • Connected game information: player identity, such as a Game Center player identity or Google Play Games player identifier, and achievement progress, when the relevant service is used.
  • Cloud saves: gameplay progress, completion records, solve times, streaks, Precision Mode results, and supported preferences sent to the platform’s cloud service when syncing is available and enabled.
  • Diagnostics and advertising information: device, performance, crash, and advertising information processed by the platform services or advertising SDKs described below. This is separate from local gameplay storage.

Achievements

On iOS, achievements use Apple Game Center. On Android, the corresponding service is Google Play Games Services. When connected, the service uses your platform player identity and achievement information to record and display achievements. Google Play Games may provide the app with a player identifier and gaming profile details, such as a gamer name and avatar, as permitted by your Google settings.

Google Play Games Services also collects its own SDK analytics and diagnostics, including SDK usage, device, and error information, to operate and improve the service. This is part of Play Games; Hexly does not add a separate analytics service. You can manage your gaming profile visibility and game sign-in choices in Google Play Games settings. See Google’s Play Games data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Cloud syncing (optional)

iOS: If iCloud is enabled for Hexly, supported gameplay data may sync through your iCloud account and Apple’s CloudKit service so it can be restored across supported Apple devices and installations. Apple manages this service.

Android: Google Play Games Saved Games is the corresponding cloud service. When available and connected, supported gameplay data is associated with the Google account selected for Hexly and can be saved and restored through Google Play Games. Manage the selected account and game sign-in through Google Play Games settings.

Android progress syncs between supported Android devices only. It is independent of iCloud and Hexly Daily. Neither mobile version syncs its progress with the browser game.

How information is used

  • To save and restore progress and preferences.
  • To recognize ownership and unlock or restore Premium and Packs.
  • To record achievements and sync supported gameplay data.
  • To provide advertising where enabled, measure advertising performance, and apply Premium’s ad-free benefits.
  • To maintain stability and performance using diagnostics provided by the included services.
  • To respond when you contact support.

Advertising

Hexly may display ads in the non-Premium experience. Hexly Premium removes ads. Android will follow the same advertising and Premium rules as iOS.

Hexly uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) for advertising on iOS, and the same platform is planned for the Android release. When ads are enabled, depending on permissions, consent, and the ads served, advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, an app-generated installation identifier where used, IP address, ad interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. This information is used to deliver and, where permitted, personalize ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its processing and privacy choices.

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission applies to tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. Where tracking permission is required, Hexly requests it before allowing that tracking. Declining tracking does not necessarily remove ads. You can change tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Android: Unity’s Android SDK disclosure includes approximate location, device or other identifiers, interactions with ads, and diagnostics. This processing supports advertising, advertising analytics, and fraud prevention; it does not add a separate analytics service to Hexly. Advertising may use the Android advertising ID where available. You can manage, reset, or delete that identifier through your device’s privacy and advertising settings, depending on the Android version. See Google’s advertising ID controls. Changing these settings does not necessarily remove ads. Apple’s tracking-permission system applies only to iOS.

In-app purchases

Optional Premium and Pack purchases use the Apple App Store on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. The store handles payment through your Apple or Google account and supplies product, transaction, or purchase-status information used by the app to recognize ownership, unlock content, and restore purchases. Nox Studios does not receive your full payment-card details through these purchase flows.

Purchases and restoration are managed by the store where the purchase was made. This does not create a shared Hexly account or automatically transfer purchases between Apple and Google.

Data sharing and security

Nox Studios does not sell your personal information. Apple processes information for App Store purchases, Game Center, iCloud, and platform diagnostics. Google processes information for Google Play purchases, Play Games achievements, saved games, and the SDK’s own analytics and diagnostics when those features are used. Advertising services receive the information described in Advertising. If you share diagnostics with developers in iOS, Apple may make crash and performance reports available to us.

Google Play Games Services encrypts data in transit using HTTPS. Unity LevelPlay also reports encrypted transmission for its SDK data. Platform services manage their own security and retention practices under their privacy policies, including Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data retention

Local gameplay data remains until it is removed from the device. Cloud saves and achievements may remain with Apple or Google until removed through their controls, subject to those services’ policies. Purchase records may be retained by the store for payment, legal, and restoration purposes. Advertising information is retained according to the advertising provider’s policy. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records.

Your choices and deleting game data

  • Local data: uninstall Hexly to remove its local app data. On Android, you can also clear the app’s storage through system Settings. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete cloud saves, achievements, or purchase records; connected services may restore data when you reinstall.
  • iOS: manage iCloud storage and Game Center through Apple’s settings. You can also change tracking permissions and whether diagnostics are shared with developers in iOS Settings.
  • Android: manage game sign-in and profile privacy through Google Play Games. Use Android’s privacy and advertising settings to manage the advertising ID used by ads. To remove Google-hosted Hexly progress and achievements, follow Google’s instructions to delete an individual game’s Play Games data and select Hexly. This is separate from clearing local app storage and does not cancel purchases or delete store transaction records.
  • Support: contact support@noxstudios.net about information you have sent directly to us. There is no separate Hexly account to delete.

Hexly Daily website

This section applies to the browser game at hexlydaily.com. Hexly Daily does not require an account and does not sync progress with either the iOS or Android app. The mobile app’s advertising, in-app purchase, achievement, and cloud-sync provisions above do not apply to Hexly Daily.

Browser storage and gameplay data

Hexly Daily stores puzzle selections, unused marks, completion state, solve times, Precision Mode results, streak history, and preferences in your browser’s local storage. This data lets the game resume puzzles, display your streaks and personal bests, and remember your settings. Moves are saved automatically when browser storage is available. The game does not send this saved progress to Nox Studios or store it in a website account.

Backups, retention, and deletion

In About your data, Download backup saves a file containing your gameplay history and preferences to your device. Restore backup reads a file you choose and restores its saved data in that browser; it does not upload the file to Nox Studios. You control where you keep or share downloaded backups.

Saved progress remains in that browser until you delete it or the browser removes it. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or changing browsers, devices, or domains can remove or separate your progress. Use Clear all local data in About your data, or your browser’s site-data controls, to remove locally saved Hexly Daily data. This does not delete downloaded backup files or affect data in the Hexly app. Nox Studios cannot recover browser saves that have been deleted.

Sharing results

Share cards and result text are created in your browser from your streak and solve results. Nothing is posted automatically. You choose whether to copy results, save an image, or share through your device’s sharing tools. If you send results to another service, that service handles them under its own privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics, and cookies

Hexly Daily does not include advertising or analytics trackers. The game uses local storage to save progress and settings, and does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Technical connection data

When you visit Hexly Daily, technical connection information, including your IP address, is processed to deliver the site and help protect against abuse. This information is separate from the puzzle progress stored in your browser.

External links

Hexly Daily includes links to the App Store, Nox Studios, and the Hexly books on Amazon, with a Google Play link to be added when Android is available. When you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy policy applies to your visit.

Support for the app and website

If you email Nox Studios, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and provide support, and retain it only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records.

Children’s privacy

The Hexly app and Hexly Daily are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via support email, contact support@noxstudios.net and we will work to remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page.

Contact

For questions, requests, or concerns about this policy, email support@noxstudios.net.
