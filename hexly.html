Hexly Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Nox Studios handles information in the Hexly iPhone and iPad app and in Hexly Daily at hexlydaily.com. If you have questions, email support@noxstudios.net.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Hexly app (iPhone and iPad)

The following app-specific provisions apply to Hexly on iPhone and iPad.

Overview

Hexly is designed to work without requiring an account. The app’s core gameplay is available without providing personal information. Where possible, data is stored on your device.

Information Hexly may store

  • Gameplay data: puzzle progress, completion state, streaks, and in-app settings.
  • Purchase status: whether Hexly Premium is active, used to unlock features and restore purchases.
  • Device-level diagnostics: if you choose to share diagnostics with developers in iOS, Apple may provide crash logs and performance information associated with Hexly.

iCloud syncing (optional)

If you have iCloud enabled for Hexly on your device, gameplay data may sync through your iCloud account so it can persist across sessions and installations. This syncing is managed by Apple’s iCloud services.

How information is used

  • To operate the app (saving progress and preferences).
  • To enable and restore in-app purchases (for example, unlocking Hexly Premium).
  • To display ads and measure performance when ads are enabled.
  • To improve stability and performance (reviewing crash logs when provided by Apple).
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.

Advertising

Hexly may display ads. Ads are served by third-party advertising partners. Depending on your settings and permissions, these partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, coarse location (if available), and app interaction data to (a) serve ads, (b) personalize ads, (c) measure performance, (d) limit fraud, and (e) support reporting. Where required, Hexly will request permission before allowing tracking for advertising purposes (for example, via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, you may still see ads, but they may be less relevant.

In-app purchases

Hexly offers optional in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Nox Studios does not receive your full payment information. Apple may provide us with purchase status (for example, whether Hexly Premium is active) so we can unlock features and restore purchases.

Data sharing

Nox Studios does not sell your personal information. When ads are enabled, limited information may be shared with advertising partners for the purposes described above. Apple may also process information as part of providing App Store and iCloud services.

Data retention

Gameplay data remains on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud account for as long as you keep Hexly installed or iCloud data available. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records.

Your choices

  • Delete app data: uninstall Hexly to remove locally stored data.
  • iCloud: you can enable or disable iCloud for Hexly in iOS Settings.
  • Tracking permission: if prompted, you can allow or deny tracking for ads, and you can change your choice in iOS Settings.
  • Diagnostics: you can control whether iOS shares diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings.

Hexly Daily website

This section applies to the browser game at hexlydaily.com. Hexly Daily does not require an account and does not sync progress with the iPhone and iPad app. The app’s advertising, in-app purchase, and iCloud provisions above do not apply to Hexly Daily.

Browser storage and gameplay data

Hexly Daily stores puzzle selections, unused marks, completion state, solve times, Precision Mode results, streak history, and preferences in your browser’s local storage. This data lets the game resume puzzles, display your streaks and personal bests, and remember your settings. Moves are saved automatically when browser storage is available. The game does not send this saved progress to Nox Studios or store it in a website account.

Backups, retention, and deletion

In About your data, Download backup saves a file containing your gameplay history and preferences to your device. Restore backup reads a file you choose and restores its saved data in that browser; it does not upload the file to Nox Studios. You control where you keep or share downloaded backups.

Saved progress remains in that browser until you delete it or the browser removes it. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or changing browsers, devices, or domains can remove or separate your progress. Use Clear all local data in About your data, or your browser’s site-data controls, to remove locally saved Hexly Daily data. This does not delete downloaded backup files or affect data in the Hexly app. Nox Studios cannot recover browser saves that have been deleted.

Sharing results

Share cards and result text are created in your browser from your streak and solve results. Nothing is posted automatically. You choose whether to copy results, save an image, or share through your device’s sharing tools. If you send results to another service, that service handles them under its own privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics, and cookies

Hexly Daily does not include advertising or analytics trackers. The game uses local storage to save progress and settings, and does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Technical connection data

When you visit Hexly Daily, technical connection information, including your IP address, is processed to deliver the site and help protect against abuse. This information is separate from the puzzle progress stored in your browser.

External links

Hexly Daily links to the App Store, Nox Studios, and the Hexly books on Amazon. When you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy policy applies to your visit.

Support for the app and website

If you email Nox Studios, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and provide support, and retain it only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records.

Children’s privacy

The Hexly app and Hexly Daily are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via support email, contact support@noxstudios.net and we will work to remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page.

Contact

For questions, requests, or concerns about this policy, email support@noxstudios.net.
