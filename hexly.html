Hexly Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Hexly handles information. If you have questions, email support@noxstudios.net.

Effective date: February 6, 2026

Overview

Hexly is designed to work without requiring an account. The app’s core gameplay is available without entering your name, email, or password. Where possible, gameplay data is stored on your device (and may optionally sync via iCloud).

Information Hexly may collect or store

  • Gameplay data: puzzle progress, completion state, streaks, stats, and in-app settings.
  • Purchase data: purchase status and receipts processed by Apple for in-app purchases.
  • Advertising data: if ads are enabled, ad networks may receive device and ad-related information such as an advertising identifier (if permitted), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction events.
  • Device-level diagnostics: crash logs and performance data if you choose to share diagnostics with developers in iOS.

Advertising

Hexly may display ads. Ads are provided by third-party partners. These partners may collect and process information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit fraud, and support reporting. If Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown, you can choose whether to allow tracking.

In-app purchases

Hexly offers in-app purchases (for example, removing ads). Purchases are processed by Apple via the AppStore. Nox Studios does not receive your full payment details. Apple may share purchase confirmation and related metadata so the app can unlock features.

iCloud syncing (optional)

If you have iCloud enabled for Hexly on your device, gameplay data may sync through your iCloud account so it can persist across devices and installations. This syncing is managed by Apple’s iCloud services.

How information is used

  • To operate the app (saving progress, preferences, and purchase status).
  • To deliver and measure ads (when ads are enabled).
  • To improve stability and performance (reviewing diagnostics when provided by Apple).
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.

Data sharing

Nox Studios does not sell your personal information. Hexly may share limited device and usage information with advertising partners when ads are enabled, and Apple may process information as part of providing AppStore and iCloud services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: if prompted, you can allow or deny tracking via App Tracking Transparency. You can also review tracking and advertising settings in iOS Settings.
  • Delete app data: uninstall Hexly to remove locally stored data.
  • iCloud: you can enable or disable iCloud for Hexly in iOS Settings.
  • Diagnostics: you can control whether iOS shares diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings.

Data retention

Gameplay data remains on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud account for as long as you keep Hexly installed or iCloud data available. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support records. Advertising partners may retain data according to their own policies.

Children’s privacy

Hexly is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via support email, contact support@noxstudios.net and we will work to remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page.

Contact

For questions, requests, or concerns about this policy, email support@noxstudios.net.

Support

We respond quickly. Include your device model and iOS version if reporting an issue.
